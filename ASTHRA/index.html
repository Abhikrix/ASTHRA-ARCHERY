About Us

Founded in 2024, Astra Archery Academy is a registered archery training center (Reg. No. 1388/TVM/24) dedicated to preserving traditional archery techniques while incorporating modern training methods.

Our academy is located in the serene surroundings of Venjaramoodu, providing the perfect atmosphere for learning this ancient sport. We pride ourselves on maintaining the highest standards of instruction and safety.

Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced archer looking to refine your skills, our certified instructors will guide you on your archery journey.
